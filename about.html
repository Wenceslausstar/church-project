Our Mission

Grace Fellowship Church was founded to help people encounter Jesus, grow in faith, and live with purpose. We believe church is more than a place to gather; it is a family where people are encouraged, equipped, and sent to serve.

Faith

We believe in the transforming power of Jesus Christ and the truth of scripture.

Community

We welcome people from every background and walk of life into a loving community.

Service

We seek to love and serve our neighbors through compassion, generosity, and action.
